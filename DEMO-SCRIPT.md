# SayItRight demo video script (about 2 minutes)

Record the screen at 1280x860 or similar. Move the mouse slowly and click
each button clearly. Read the lines below as voiceover, or show them as
captions.

## Scene 1 - the problem (0:00 to 0:20)

Show the empty Check screen.

Say: "We have all sent a message that sounded fine in our head and landed
badly. SayItRight checks how your message sounds before you send it. The
AI is a small machine learning model that runs fully in your browser. No
account, no server, nothing you type leaves your device."

## Scene 2 - a harsh message (0:20 to 0:50)

Click "Sample messages", then click the "Angry group chat message" card.
Back on the Check screen, click "Check tone".

Say: "Here is an angry group chat message. The model calls it Harsh, 98
percent, and shows the words that drove the score: useless, nobody,
wasting. Below, it suggests softer wording and builds a softened version I
can copy."

Click "Use this version", then "Check tone" again.

Say: "If I use the softer version and check again, the tone improves."

## Scene 3 - a polite email (0:50 to 1:10)

Click "Sample messages", then "Email to a lecturer", then "Check tone".

Say: "A courteous email to a lecturer reads as Polite, and the top words
are please, thank, grateful, sir. That is exactly the impression I want to
give."

## Scene 4 - teaching the model (1:10 to 1:45)

Click "Teach the model". Point at the training set stats.

Say: "This is my favourite part. The model learns from labelled examples,
125 to start with. I can teach it new words. Wahala is a Nigerian word for
trouble, and the model has never seen it."

Type into the message box: "This wahala is too much, I am tired of wahala."
Choose Harsh, click "Add and retrain". Add one or two more wahala
examples the same way.

Say: "Now watch the Harsh count grow. The model has retrained."

Go back to "Check a message", type "That was pure wahala, wahala
everywhere." and click "Check tone".

Say: "Before teaching, the model was unsure about that sentence. Now it
calls it Harsh with high confidence, and wahala is the top word. The model
really learned."

## Scene 5 - honesty and close (1:45 to 2:05)

Type: "Oh great, another delay. Just what I needed today." Click "Check
tone".

Say: "It is not perfect. Sarcasm like this still fools it, because it
counts words on their own. I would rather show that honestly than pretend
otherwise. SayItRight: check how your message sounds, before you send it."

End on the logo in the sidebar.
